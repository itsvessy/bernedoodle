# Preventive Calendar and Routines

> [!summary]
> A preventive calendar turns good intentions into repeatable care actions and cleaner vet visits.

> [!warning]
> Exact intervals for preventives must come from your veterinarian and local risk profile.

## Suggested Owner Calendar Structure
| Interval | Owner Tasks |
|---|---|
| Daily | Intake, stool, behavior, and activity notes |
| Weekly | Weight/body condition, coat/skin/paw checks |
| Monthly | Preventive log review and supply verification |
| Quarterly | Risk review: travel, boarding, exposure changes |
| Annual | Full preventive plan reset with veterinarian |

## Calendar Setup Steps
1. Enter all current due dates from vet records.
2. Add pre-alert reminders 7 days before each item.
3. Log completion and reactions immediately.
4. Review missed tasks monthly.
