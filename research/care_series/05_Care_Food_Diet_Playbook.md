# Care, Food, and Diet Playbook

> [!summary]
> This playbook gives a practical feeding system: set baseline, track weekly, adjust in small steps, and escalate early when GI or weight changes persist.

> [!warning]
> Rapid weight loss, repeated vomiting, blood in stool, no appetite >24h, or dehydration signs require prompt veterinary assessment.

## Food Decision Workflow
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Record current food, amount, schedule, treat pattern |
| Monitor | Track body condition, stool quality, appetite, energy weekly |
| Adjust | Change one variable at a time in small increments |
| Reassess | Review 7-14 day response before further changes |

## Body Condition Practical Scale (Owner Version)
| Observation | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Ribs very hard to feel, no visible waist | Possible over-condition trend |
| Ribs felt with light pressure, waist visible | Likely target condition zone |
| Ribs prominent, muscle loss concerns | Under-condition concern |

## GI Event Response Plan
1. Stop all nonessential diet changes.
2. Log exact onset and progression.
3. Preserve hydration monitoring.
4. Escalate when signs persist or worsen.

## Treat and Extra Calories Control
- Keep a written treat budget.
- Use consistent treat types during training blocks.
- Recalculate total intake if rewards increase.
