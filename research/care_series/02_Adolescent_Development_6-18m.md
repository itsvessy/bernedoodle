# Adolescent Development (6-18 months)

> [!summary]
> Expect inconsistency in behavior and impulse control. Success in this stage comes from repetition, not intensity.

> [!warning]
> New collapse, exercise intolerance, persistent cough, or severe behavior change should be escalated to a veterinarian.

## Stage Priorities
| Domain | Owner Focus |
|---|---|
| Training reliability | Daily reps of recall, leash behavior, settle cue |
| Arousal control | Planned decompression after stimulation |
| Body condition | Monitor growth drift and adjust routines promptly |
| Household consistency | One set of rules across all family members |

## Behavior Regression Plan
1. Reduce task difficulty when errors spike.
2. Rebuild in smaller steps.
3. Increase rewards for calm behavior.
4. End sessions before frustration rises.

## Activity Progression Template
| Week | Physical Load | Mental Load |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Maintain baseline walks/sniffing | Add short puzzle/obedience blocks |
| 3-4 | Add moderate-duration session | Add controlled novelty exposure |
| 5-6 | Maintain one higher-effort day, one lighter day | Add advanced impulse drills |

## Decision Prompts for Vet Discussion
- Is current growth pattern appropriate for this dog?
- What signs should trigger activity reduction?
- Which symptoms warrant same-day versus emergency care?
