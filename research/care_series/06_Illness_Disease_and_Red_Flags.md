# Illness, Disease, and Red Flags

> [!summary]
> Use this triage structure to decide urgency and communicate clearly with your veterinary team.

> [!warning]
> ER now signs: collapse, severe breathing effort, nonproductive retching with distress, pale/gray gums, uncontrolled bleeding, repeated seizures.

## Triage Decision Table
| Tier | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ER now | Collapse, severe respiratory effort, repeated nonproductive retching, severe trauma | Go to emergency clinic immediately |
| Same-day vet | Persistent vomiting/diarrhea, fever signs, acute lameness, eye pain, sustained lethargy | Call for same-day appointment |
| Monitor + scheduled visit | Mild transient signs that rapidly improve | Monitor closely and schedule if recurrence occurs |

## Symptom Cluster Matrix
| Cluster | What To Record |
|---|---|
| Respiratory | Cough frequency, rest breathing pattern, trigger context |
| GI | Stool consistency, vomiting count, appetite trend |
| Mobility | Onset of lameness, affected limb, recovery time |
| Behavior/cognition | Sleep disruption, disorientation, reactivity shifts |

## Escalation Rules
1. Any worsening trend over 24 hours moves up one urgency tier.
2. Recurrent episodes move up one urgency tier.
3. Multi-system signs (for example GI + breathing) move to urgent assessment.

## Vet Call Script Template
- "Symptom started at:"
- "Current trend compared to yesterday:"
- "Videos/photos available: yes/no"
- "Current food/medications/supplements:"
