# Senior Care (7+ years)

> [!summary]
> Senior care emphasizes quality of life, mobility support, closer monitoring cadence, and lower thresholds for veterinary escalation.

> [!warning]
> Sudden confusion, collapse, breathing changes, pain vocalization, or inability to stand is urgent.

## Senior Monitoring Grid
| Domain | Daily Check |
|---|---|
| Mobility | Rising, stairs, gait smoothness |
| Cognition | Night waking, disorientation, interaction changes |
| Comfort | Restlessness, guarding behavior, pain signs |
| Intake | Appetite and water pattern |

## Comfort-Oriented Routine
1. Shorter, more frequent activity blocks.
2. Predictable sleep/wake rhythm.
3. Lower-slip home surfaces and easier access points.
4. More frequent owner logging and trend review.

## Escalation Triggers
| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| New collapse/faint episode | Emergency care now |
| 3+ days appetite decline | Same-day vet call |
| Rapid behavior/cognition shift | Priority vet evaluation |
| Persistent pain behavior | Veterinary pain workup discussion |
