# Appendix A: Food Transition Template

> [!summary]
> Use this template to run controlled food transitions and quickly identify intolerance trends.

> [!warning]
> Stop and call your veterinarian for severe GI signs, blood in stool, persistent vomiting, or lethargy.

## Decision Rules
| Pattern | Action |
|---|---|
| Mild transient soft stool only | Hold current ratio 24-48h |
| Worsening GI trend | Step back one transition level |
| Severe GI symptoms | Stop transition and call vet |
